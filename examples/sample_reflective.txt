When I started the group project I was not sure what my role was. I took notes in the first meetings, and that turned out to matter more than I expected, because the team kept coming back to them when we disagreed about what we had decided.

Looking back, I would speak up earlier. I waited until the third week to say that the schedule was unrealistic, and by then we had lost time. Next time I will raise concerns as soon as I notice them.
